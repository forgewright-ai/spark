Introducing the Feathertouch Mini

The Feathertouch Mini is a matchbox-sized controller, wired or wireless.
It comes in two models: the Mini with Ethernet for $39, and the Mini W,
wireless, for $42. In packs of three they drop to $33 and $36 per unit.

The case is aluminium, about the footprint of a matchbox. It captures
video at 1080p, presents keyboard and mouse over USB, and runs the same
web interface as the full Feathertouch. Firmware is open source from day
one, and the cloud and updates carry over unchanged.
