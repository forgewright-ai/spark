The workshop's summer lunch is this Friday, 12 June, from noon to two,
in the yard behind the workshop. Everyone who works here is invited,
both shifts, and it is for staff only. There is nothing to sign and no
one needs to bring anything: the workshop pays, and the office has
arranged the food, the drinks, the tables and the chairs. There is a
meat dish and a vegetarian one, and a card beside each lists what is
in it. The yard is covered if it rains. The morning shift stops at
noon, the two hours count as worked time for both shifts, and the
lunch ends at two so the afternoon shift starts on time.
