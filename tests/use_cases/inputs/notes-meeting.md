# Website relaunch -- planning meeting, Oct 21

Present: Marta (marketing), Jun (engineering), Felipe (design), Sara (support)

## Decisions

- The relaunch moves from November 3 to November 17, to give QA two
  more weeks on the checkout flow.
- We keep the current hosting provider for launch and look at moving
  after the holidays.
- The new pricing page ships with the relaunch, not before.
- Support gets a one-page FAQ about the new account menu before launch.

## Discussion

Jun said the checkout rewrite is feature complete, but the payment
provider's sandbox was down for most of last week, so the refund path
has only been tested by hand. Felipe showed the final mobile designs;
everyone liked the shorter signup form. Marta wants a launch email to
the newsletter list on the day, and a blog post the week after.

Sara asked who will write the help center articles for the new
dashboard. Nobody owns it yet. We talked about whether the old URLs
should redirect to the new pages or show a notice; no decision.

The budget for the launch campaign came up: Marta asked for $8,000,
finance has approved $5,000 so far.

- Felipe: hand off the icon set to engineering.
- Marta: draft the launch email.
