Title: City council approves bike lanes on three West Side streets
Feed: Riverside Gazette -- Local
Date: Wed, 21 Oct 2026 18:20:00 -0500
Link: https://gazette.example.net/local/2026/10/21/bike-lanes

The city council voted 9 to 4 on Tuesday night to build protected bike
lanes on Fulton Avenue, Lake Street and Grand Boulevard, after a
two-hour public hearing that drew more than 80 speakers.

The $6.2 million project will replace one lane of car traffic on each
street with a lane separated by concrete curbs. Construction is planned
in two phases: Fulton and Lake in spring 2027, Grand in the fall.

Supporters pointed to city data showing 14 serious crashes involving
cyclists on the three streets since 2023. Several business owners on
Grand Boulevard spoke against the plan, saying the loss of parking
would hurt deliveries; the council added an amendment that keeps
loading zones on every block.

Council member Rosa Delgado, who sponsored the measure, said the city
will count bike and car traffic for a year after each phase opens and
report the results publicly.

The transportation department expects to finish detailed designs by
February.
