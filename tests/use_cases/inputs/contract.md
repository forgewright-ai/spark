# Independent Contractor Services Agreement

This Independent Contractor Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made
on September 2, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Northwind Analytics
LLC, an Illinois limited liability company with its principal office at
410 North Wells Street, Suite 900, Chicago, Illinois 60654 (the
"Client"), and Rafael Moreira, an individual doing business as Moreira
Data Studio (the "Contractor"). The Client and the Contractor are each a
"Party" and together the "Parties".

## 1. Services

1.1 The Contractor will design, build and document a reporting pipeline
for the Client's retail sales data, as described in the statement of
work attached as Exhibit A (the "Services").

1.2 The Contractor decides the means, method and manner of performing
the Services. The Client may give reasonable direction about the
results it needs, but not about how the Contractor does the work.

1.3 The Contractor will provide the Services remotely, except for up to
two on-site workshops at the Client's Chicago office, which the Parties
will schedule at least ten business days in advance.

1.4 Any change to the scope of the Services must be agreed in a written
change order signed by both Parties. A change order states the added
work, the estimated hours and any change to the delivery dates.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until the
Services are complete, unless it ends earlier under Section 8.

2.2 The Parties expect the Services to be complete by January 30, 2027.
A missed estimate is not by itself a breach of this Agreement.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Client will pay the Contractor a fee of $85 per hour for time
spent performing the Services.

3.2 The Contractor will invoice the Client monthly, on or about the
first business day of each month, for the hours worked in the previous
month. Each invoice lists the hours by day and by task.

3.3 Payment is due net 30 from the invoice date. The Client will pay by
bank transfer to the account named on the invoice.

3.4 Any amount still unpaid 30 days after its due date bears interest of
1.5% per month, or the highest rate the law allows if that is lower,
from the due date until it is paid.

3.5 If an invoice is more than 45 days overdue, the Contractor may pause
the Services after giving five business days' written notice. The
delivery dates move by the length of the pause.

3.6 The Client may dispute an invoice in good faith by writing to the
Contractor within 15 days of receiving it, naming the items it disputes.
The Client will pay the undisputed part by the due date.

## 4. Expenses

4.1 The Client will reimburse reasonable travel expenses for the on-site
workshops in Section 1.3, at cost and with receipts.

4.2 Any single expense over $200 requires the Client's prior written
approval. Software licences bought for the Services remain the Client's
property and are billed at cost.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1 All reports, code, documentation and other materials the Contractor
creates for the Client under this Agreement (the "Work Product") are
assigned to the Client upon full payment of the fees for that Work
Product.

5.2 The Contractor keeps ownership of its pre-existing tools, libraries
and know-how ("Contractor Tools"). Where Contractor Tools are part of
the Work Product, the Contractor grants the Client a perpetual,
non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use them with the Work Product.

5.3 The Contractor may describe the project in general terms in its
portfolio, without naming the Client's data or customers, unless the
Client objects in writing.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 Each Party will keep the other Party's confidential information
secret and use it only to perform or receive the Services.

6.2 Confidential information does not include information that is
public through no fault of the receiving Party, that the receiving
Party already knew, or that it receives lawfully from a third party.

6.3 These duties last for three years after this Agreement ends, and
for trade secrets as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 7. Independent Contractor Status

7.1 The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee,
partner or agent of the Client. The Contractor is responsible for its
own taxes, insurance and equipment.

7.2 The Contractor will carry professional liability insurance of at
least $1,000,000 per claim for the term of this Agreement and will show
proof of it on request.

## 8. Termination

8.1 Either Party may end this Agreement for convenience by giving the
other Party 14 days' written notice.

8.2 Either Party may end this Agreement at once by written notice if the
other Party commits a material breach and does not cure it within 10
days of receiving written notice describing the breach.

8.3 When this Agreement ends for any reason, the Client will pay for all
Services performed and approved expenses incurred up to the end date,
and the Contractor will deliver all Work Product completed so far.

8.4 Sections 5, 6, 9, 10 and 11 survive the end of this Agreement.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental or
consequential damages, including lost profits, even if told they were
possible.

9.2 Each Party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the
fees paid by the Client in the six months before the claim arose. The
cap does not apply to a breach of Section 6.

## 10. Non-Solicitation

10.1 During this Agreement and for 12 months after it ends, neither
Party will hire or try to hire an employee of the other Party who
worked on the Services, without the other Party's written consent.

## 11. General

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois,
without regard to its conflict of laws rules. The state and federal
courts in Cook County, Illinois, have exclusive jurisdiction.

11.2 Notices must be in writing and sent by email with a copy by
courier to the addresses above. A notice is received when the courier
delivers it.

11.3 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's
written consent, except to a successor of its whole business.

11.4 This Agreement, with its exhibits, is the whole agreement between
the Parties about the Services and replaces any earlier proposal.

11.5 If a court finds any part of this Agreement unenforceable, the rest
stays in effect.

## Exhibit A -- Statement of Work

A.1 Discovery: two weeks of interviews with the Client's sales and
finance teams, and a written summary of the reports they need.

A.2 Pipeline: nightly loading of point-of-sale exports into the
Client's warehouse, with checks that stop a load when a store's totals
do not reconcile.

A.3 Reports: a weekly sales report by store and by product family, and
a monthly margin report.

A.4 Handover: a runbook, one recorded training session, and four weeks
of email support after delivery.
